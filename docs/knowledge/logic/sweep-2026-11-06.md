# Logic land sweep: the audit, 2026-11-06

By the Maths Teacher (math-teacher), for the owner's report from the installed app: *"The riddles island lessons are not good. I saw some wrong answer drawings (some duplicates) and the questions themselves aren't written properly."* Branch `logic-sweep` (from `hebrew-review`, PR 39). **Audit only: nothing is fixed yet.**

## How it was checked

- **Every activity was seen as the child sees it.** `app/test/qa_logic_audit_test.dart` (off in CI; run with `--dart-define=LOGIC_AUDIT=true --dart-define=LOGIC_AUDIT_OUT=<folder>`) opens each of the 135 logic activities (LOG-A-01 to A-10, LOG-B-01, 02, 04, 06, 08, 09, 11) in the real `LessonScreen` on the phone board 852 x 393, right to left with the real fonts, and writes one PNG per activity, named `<skill>__<id>.png`. It plays the **current sources** in `content/starter` (the signed pack 0.10.0 is older: all 135 logic files differ from it), so what is shown is what a sweep would change. Every PNG was opened and judged.
- Every question, hint and feedback line was read (dump of all 135 items).
- A script checked every pattern row: exactly one option continues or fills the row periodically, exactly one cell of each spot-the-error row can be changed to make the row periodic, and how many whole units come before the gap. All rows have a unique answer; two have fewer than two units before the gap (D16).
- Logic rows are drawn left to right with the "?" at the right end, as decided on 2026-10-30 (`docs/design/b40-visual-hints.md` F5, §8a). Note for the curriculum-expert: `docs/PLAN.md` decision 7 and `docs/curriculum.md` §8.6 still say pattern rows run right to left; they should be brought in line (not counted as a content defect).

### The 5 worst

1. **b.logb11.1 to .3, ordering towers: the answer is on the screen.** The palette is laid out already sorted; in b.logb11.2 the child copies it left to right and is right (D9). And it then says "you copied the order" (D38). PNG `LOG-B-11__b.logb11.2.png`.
2. **The duck and the bird are one drawing in two colours** (D1). In b15.loga01.6 ("only ducks: what came by mistake?") and log.odd.bird the odd one is only a different colour, so LOG-A-01/02 teach "look at colour", the opposite of the skill. PNG `LOG-A-01__b15.loga01.6.png`, `LOG-A-02__log.odd.bird.png`.
3. **Size answers that look like duplicates** (D2, D3). Tiles are drawn bigger than the row, so the medium tile equals the row's big one (b15.loga06.1), the right "big" tile is hardly bigger than the row's medium (log.size.last), and towers 4, 5 and 6 are the same striped stack (b.logb04.5). This is most likely the "duplicates" the owner saw. PNG `LOG-A-06__b15.loga06.1.png`, `LOG-A-10__log.size.last.png`, `LOG-B-04__b.logb04.5.png`.
4. **b.logb04.7 and .8, "the towers grow": two terms, a +2 rule, and feedback that says +1** (D11, D37, D44). 1, 3, ? has more than one fair answer, the wrong line states a false rule, and both distractors carry the wrong tag. PNG `LOG-B-04__b.logb04.7.png`.
5. **LOG-A-08 questions about a picture that is not there** (D14, D29). "יָצָא אֶפְרוֹחַ. מָה הָיָה מִיָּד לִפְנֵי זֶה?" over an empty meadow; "מַה קּוֹרֶה קֹדֶם?" with no story on the screen (7 of 8 items). PNG `LOG-A-08__b15.loga08.1.png`, `LOG-A-08__log.story.egg.first.png`.

Close behind: the 8-cell spot-the-error rows of LOG-B-02 where every picture is about 12 px (D20, `LOG-B-02__b.logb02.2.png`), and "look for the middle" said on first/last questions (D36, `LOG-A-10__b15.loga10.1.png`).
