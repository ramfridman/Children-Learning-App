## 0. The rules this slice keeps

- **Every letter is taught whole** (D16, frame §5.3): each letter opens with one LS02 `intro` (shape; its sound in a key word with a picture, a stop heard inside a CV unit, D21; its name as a separate fact; watch it drawn; one trace; find it), and then the four facets (name, sound, writing, reading) are practised mixed. Blending always uses the sound, never the name (בָּ, never "bet-kamats").
- **Consonant-plus-vowel units come in the vowel order a, i, o, u, e, shva.** This slice is step S1: the only vowel is **a** (ָ and ַ, one sound, two signs) and the final ָה. Every written face in the slice uses only S1 letters and signs (checked by script, §13). A word heard (a picture's name) may have any vowel; a word shown may not.
- **Look-alike letters are taught apart in time.** S1 holds none of the syllabus's confusable pairs (ב/כ, ד/ר, ה/ח/ת, ו/ז/ן, ס/ם, ע/צ, ג/נ, ט/מ). No letter of a later group is ever shown in this slice, not even as a distractor. The look-alike partners appear only as **stroke classifiers** (a ר drawn with a sharp corner is a ד), whose re-teach shows the child's own letter and never names the untaught one (§5.4).
- **Nikud is hand-written here and checked** by the editor and by script (§13); nothing is generated at runtime. Letter faces are bare letters with only the S1 marks (the dagesh of בּ, the dot of שׁ).
- **The children are addressed in the plural** (`hebrew-notes.md` §9.1): plural imperatives (`גְּעוּ`, `כִּתְבוּ`, `קִרְאוּ`), "let's" (`בּוֹאוּ נִפְגֹּשׁ`), the impersonal present (`מַתְחִילִים כָּאן`). No line gives the voice a gender. Letters are feminine (`זוֹ`, `הִיא`), and no preposition is put on a letter name (it would change its dot).
- **Ordinary words stay in** (the owner kept טִיל): שַׁבָּת, שׁוֹר, חוֹמָה and the rest are everyday words a child of 5 knows.
- **Band B presentation** (frame §3.1): at most 4 choices (this slice uses 3; the pairs item has 4 pairs, the tap-all a row of 5, the wheel 4 parts), 5 to 8 items an activity, no timer, no speed.
- **Every distractor is tagged** with a tag of this course; every choice item has 3 choices (two tagged distractors; the same tag may serve both when one misconception explains both, frame §2.3). There is no 2-choice item in the slice. Free-response modes (LS02, LS06) use classifiers; an error they cannot name is a retry with no tag.
- **Speech is never scored.** No item here uses the microphone.
- **Voices later.** Every spoken thing has a clip id (§9). The D2 question (can a voice say an isolated sound cleanly?) is open, so an item names a **sound clip id**, never a form; the clip's row gives the isolated form and the fallback (L5 §7.4).
