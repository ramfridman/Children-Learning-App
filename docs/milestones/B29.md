## Owner decisions already made (2026-10-29)

1. Birth MONTH and YEAR (no day) are kept on the device only; the age in months is computed there and never stored or sent.
2. The parent-confirmed SCHOOL STAGE (`gan3, gan4, gan5, grade1, grade2, grade3`) is the only new age value that syncs, beside the band.
3. Presentation (how things look) follows age; content (which lessons) follows mastery per land.
4. Four presentation stages from the age in months: P1 up to 3;5, P2 3;6 to 4;11, P3 5;0 to 6;5, P4 6;6 and up. Reading settings (captions, nikud, amount of text) follow the school stage the parent confirms, not the age.

## 3. What changes in the plan

### 3.1 The school stage sets the band

| School stage | Band (synced, for the server) | Usual presentation stage (from the month and year, device only) |
|---|---|---|
| `gan3` | A | P1 or P2 |
| `gan4` | A | P2 |
| `gan5` (gan hova) | B | P3 |
| `grade1` | B | P3, then P4 from 6;6 |
| `grade2`, `grade3` | C | P4 |

A child older than grade 3 stays `grade3` (Band C). A parent who gives no month and year gets the middle of the band.

### 3.2 The start of a land uses the same staircase as a language course

Step 6 of the old plan ("an age-based starting prior") becomes: **the lands' warm-up is the staircase the languages already use** (`engine/staircase.dart`), with the land's units as levels (A1 to C4), and the **school stage sets the first level** (the start of the band's middle unit, minus one, curriculum §10.1). Then the answers decide. This one change fixes the open part of B-15 (one lucky answer), gives age its one say in content, and keeps one rule for lands and courses. It is M10's step 0.3 too.

### 3.3 How age fit applies to the language courses

| Thing | Rule with B-29 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A course's presentation band | As today: the parent's choice for the course, else the band from the school stage | The course reads the band it reads today |
| Inside that band | The presentation stage (P1 to P4, device only) sets choice caps, items per activity, session length and hint pace, exactly as in the lands | One look per child, whatever the subject |
| A gate's minimum age (English oral gate, Russian letters before 5) | The age in months on the device when the parent gave month and year; otherwise the band's age (3, 5, 7) as today. The result never leaves the device | "Not before 5" should open at 5;0, not at the start of Band B |
| Where a course starts | The questionnaire and the LS13 staircase only. **Age never sets a language start** | Knowing English or Russian does not follow from age |
| The Hebrew frame's captions | The reading setting from the school stage, as in the lands | Decision 4 |
| Target-language text | The course rules stay: no letters under Band A presentation (except Hebrew A-10 to A-12); captions after the answer by route | `docs/languages/frame.md` §3.1 |

### 3.4 Today's Adventure

The school stage chooses the row of `AdventureParams` and nothing else. The rows stay equal until child testing gives a reason to differ.

### 3.5 Ties to M10 (Band C)

- **P4 (6;6 and up) is the Band C look.** Band C content is reached by mastery, never by age, and a 7-year-old on Band A content gets the P4 look.
- `grade2` and `grade3` send Band C to the server; the staircase starts them near the end of Band B, so they reach Band C within the first warm-up if they know Band B.
- **M10 cannot ship before the path gate** (M10 E0): without it, a young child who finishes Band B would be walked into Band C.
- Content order: the old step 7 ("the rest of Band B, then Band C") is replaced by M10, which builds the 24 missing A and B skills that sit under Band C, plus a small "Band B completion" pack for the other 9. Because the app is for any child, the order follows the skill graph, not one family's ages. The young ramp (under 3;6) stays a small separate batch.
