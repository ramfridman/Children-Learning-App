## B-24 The microphone only where speaking is the answer

- Rule: `voiceAnswer: true` only if the child must produce a word the prompt does not already say (number, colour, shape, day, time), saying it shows the same skill as a tap, and the options sound different. "Where is the number x?" fails the first test.
- Result on pack 0.9.0: 258 of 767 activities keep the mic (tap_answer 218, count_tap 28, flash 12) and 509 lose it (QA-21 narrowed the first 267 by the 9 NUM-B-16 "teen is ten and how many" items, whose answer is the prompt said back). The mic is not drawn at all when false. Give-N, balance and clock-set are false.
- Code (app-developer): `voiceAnswer` in `activity/v1`, written by `tools/author.py` and `band_b*.py` (template default plus per-kind overrides), validator errors and a warning, `_mic` null unless voice is on and the flag is true, early accept not firing on a number that starts another option's number word (8 activities). New pack version (0.9.0) and signature.
- Found on the way: the matcher treats any "לא" as a negation, so a yes/no question could never take "no"; the 25 yes/no activities lose their mic.
- QA: pack test (exactly 258 true, per-template and per-skill counts), distinct accept-lists, no `lesson-mic` when false, prefix tests, phone check. Design-director checks the layout without a mic.
