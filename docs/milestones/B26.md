Status: PLAN, waiting for the owner's OK. Branch `b26-nitzi-home` (own worktree, cut from `main` `48d7df0`). Details: `docs/design/nitzi-home.md` (design-director), `docs/agents/b26-hebrew.md` (hebrew-text-editor), `docs/milestones/B26-dev-assessment.md` (app-developer). ## What the owner gets

1. **The sign of the home island is named after the child:** `הָאִי שֶׁל <name>` (no name set: `הָאִי שֶׁלִּי`). The name is shown as the parent typed it, on screen only, and never goes to a vendor (no new path; the sign is computed on the device).
2. **Tapping the sign (or the island's palm side, not the companion) opens the child's home**, a close-up of the island with: the bottle (today's adventure, the one obvious next step), the companion at its house door who greets (waves once, nothing timed), a book (stickers) and a lighthouse (the Trophy Hall), and a back pebble to the map in the same place as on the other screens. The map keeps its scroll position on return.
3. **Phone, phoneSE, tablet landscape and tablet portrait** each get a layout (tap targets at least 112, gaps at least 24 on phones and 60 on tablets).

## Found while planning (must not be missed)

- Nitzi must **not** be added to `_openableLands()`: `_openLand` does `TrophyLand.values.byName(...)` and `TrophyLand` has no `nitzi`, so the app would crash. The sign gets its own `onOpenHome` callback.
- The companion covers most of Nitzi's tap area (free strip about 89 pt on phone, 74 on SE, 110 on tablet portrait), hence "the sign and the palm side open home".
- All four home goldens change, including `home_tablet_landscape.png` (B-23 required it byte-identical; this time the sign text changes it). Three tests that say "Nitzi has no way in" are flipped.
- B-27 gets: the SE "+" pebble disc runs about 2 pt past the right edge; the pill announces a button without a tap (fixed here by decision 2).
- Security: no change to sync, packs, gateway, logs or new input, so the security-engineer's review is not required; an optional short look at the new on-screen name surface (direction marks are stripped from the name).
