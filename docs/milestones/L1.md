# L1: Text, direction and fonts (F1, F5, LS01, count-and-tap in a course)

**Status:** the owner approved the whole Languages frame on 2026-11-03, with the order of the milestones (`docs/languages/frame.md` section 8, "there is a lot of work to do even without the voicing, so let's start": L0 and L1 start now). That approval covers this plan. Branch `languages-l1`, cut from `languages-foundation` (which holds L0).

This plan follows `docs/languages/frame.md` (section 2.1 F1, F2 and F5; section 2.2; section 2.3 LS01 and the two-choice rule; section 6 L1) and `docs/languages/fonts.md` (section 6 layout rules, section 8 `TargetText`). The fonts themselves (F2: Nitznutz Letters, `TargetText`, `targetStyle`) were built before L0 and are not touched here.

## 1. Goal and "done when"

A language activity of the existing tap template (LS01) can be authored, validated and played with the text of its language in the right direction and font, with a sound on each option, with two-clip lines, and with the caption after the answer; the validator knows the language rules of F5; and count-and-tap in a course is practice with one scored step. No content pack and no real voice: everything is tested on fixture packs and with fake clips.

Acceptance criteria, reported one by one at the end:

1. **F1, direction-aware text runs.** A course has a reading direction (`eng`, `rus` left to right; `heb`, and the lands, right to left). The row of answer tiles of a language activity, the pictures on its stage and the hint geometry follow the course's direction, not the screen's. A target word is always an isolated left-to-right run (`TargetText`); a prompt never prints it inline: a word that must show sits in its own chip on its own line (`TargetChip`, shown under the guide's bubble through `Spoken.show`), and a Hebrew line that holds a target word is refused by the validator. Numbers and arrows are never mirrored.
2. **F5, the validator's language checks** (each tested on fixture packs, one test per rule, and each message names the file and the path):
   - script purity per course (`eng` Latin only; `rus` Cyrillic with ё, and U+0301 only after a vowel and never after ё; `heb` Hebrew letters and nikud);
   - no look-alike letter from another script (a Cyrillic а in an English word, a Latin o in a Russian one);
   - no Latin or Cyrillic in a Hebrew frame line (a caption or a title, in any pack);
   - decodable words: a skill that declares `letters` offers only words made of the graphemes taught by it and the skills below it (or its taught tricky words);
   - every distractor tagged, and the frame section 2.3 rule (a 3-choice item has at least 2 tagged distractors, a 2-choice item 1; 2-choice items are at most half of a skill's scored items; a skill with 2-choice items has 3-choice items too, or is binary and balanced; a check is never a lone 2-choice item);
   - no accent-kind tag on a distractor;
   - every tag defined once in its course's catalogue (the slug of a tag is unique inside the course, whatever its strand; every tag used is defined; a language tag has a kind).
3. **LS01 on `tap_answer` 1.3.** Text tiles; a speaker on a tile that plays the option's clip (a tile with no clip still works and shows its text); sound-only tiles (a tap plays the clip and answers); the caption after the answer (a pure rule per course, route and presentation band, never under Band A); two-clip lines (a Hebrew frame clip, then the target clip) for every spoken line; option text through `TargetText` (Heebo for Hebrew); the tile widths and the hint geometry from one function, on the four boards, using the existing tile tokens.
4. **Count-and-tap in a course (frame section 2.2).** `count_tap` 1.1: in a language course the counting taps play a number clip each and are practice (nothing is written, no tag); a double tap lights the object and starts the count again; the final "how many?" step is scored, on sound-only tiles.
5. Analyzer clean, format clean, the whole Flutter suite green, goldens for LS01 on the four boards looked at; PLAN.md updated.

## 2. The decisions this plan makes

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 | New optional option fields: `text` (the tile's text, in the course's language), `clip` (a `vo:` reference, the option's own sound), `reveal` (the word shown under the picture after the answer). Text and clip-only options are allowed in language packs only. | Frame LS01 a, b, c and g. |
| D-2 | New optional fields on **every** spoken line: `target` (a `vo:` reference, the target clip that follows the Hebrew frame clip) and `show` (target text for the chip). | F3 as far as LS01 needs it, and F1's chip. |
| D-3 | The caption's field is `reveal`, not `caption`, because every key named `caption` in a pack is a Hebrew line (the Hebrew checks read it). | A target word in a `caption` would be refused. |
| D-4 | A sound-only tile answers on one tap and plays its clip as it does. The replay and a hint play all the sounds of the tiles in turn. | The English and Russian batches: "each plays its number word on a tap; the child taps the one that says how many", and "the buttons play again" at level 1. |
| D-5 | `answer.pole` (`yes`, `no`, `same`, `different`) and `answer.pairWith` (an activity id) say which side of a two-way skill an item is and which item it is paired with in a check. Skill field `binary`. | The balance rule and the balanced pair of frame section 2.3 need them. The engine's side of the pair ("passes only if both are right") is a later step. |
| D-6 | Skill fields `graphemes` (what the skill teaches) and `tricky` (whole words taught as they are). The existing bool `letters` stays "this skill is letter work" and switches the decodable check on. | `letters` already exists (L0) and means letter work. |
| D-7 | The misconception document gets `kind` (`diagnostic`, `on_the_way`, `accent`, `practice`); a language pack must give it. | The accent check needs it; frame section 4.1 defines the kinds. |
| D-8 | `count_tap` in a course: `scene.objects.clips` (one number clip for each tap); `traceClassifiers` must be empty. | A counting error is maths, not language (frame section 2.2); a core tag may not appear in a course. |
| D-9 | Digits are not allowed in the text of `eng` and `rus` tiles. | One script per item, and never a digit among look-alike letters (fonts.md section 6). Numerals in Hebrew letter work are drawn by other templates. |
| D-10 | The caption policy (`captionAfterAnswer`) is a pure function of course, route, presentation band and a parent switch. The parent switch has no screen and no setting yet. | Frame LS01 g: "a parent can turn it off". A setting needs `settings-put` and a deploy, so it waits for the parent page of that course. |

## 4. Order of work and checks

Parts (a) to (d) are built in that order, each with its tests and its own commit. After each part: `flutter analyze`, `dart format --set-exit-if-changed .` and the affected tests. At the end: the whole `flutter test` once. The gateway is not touched (no Deno change, no migration).

## 5. Security items (for the security-engineer's review before merge)

1. **Rule 2, ids and enums only.** The new fields are pack content (signed), not device input. No new input path from the child: a tap answers with an option id, as before; a sound-only tile adds no text to the response code.
2. **Rule 5.** The new target text is authored and shown as plain text; no model output reaches it (the tutor is off for every language item, L0).
3. **Packs.** The new checks only add refusals; a pack that passed before still passes (the starter pack is checked by its own test). The new fields are optional, so the starter pack's bytes are unchanged.
4. **Child safety.** No audio is recorded or sent; the clips are the pack's own. No timer, countdown or streak is added to the child's space (the caption and the chip are not timed).
5. No new package, no new host, no change to `network_hosts.json`.

## 6. What is deferred, and why

- **A language lesson on the lesson screen.** `LessonScreen` still loads the one starter pack (`contentProvider`); a lesson of a language pack, its voice bank (the language's teaching voice, L2) and the child's route and presentation band wait for the course milestones. The two-clip line and the clip hook are wired in `LessonScreen`; the caption policy is called with its defaults (off) until then.
- **The parent switch for the caption** (D-10).
- **The engine's balanced pair** (a pair of checks that passes only when both are right): the validator checks the pair; the session builder serving them together is L4.
- **LS01 modes d, e, f** (a text stem, a cloze, a sequence) and the text faces of `match_pairs`, `sort_drag`, `spot_error` and `build_pattern`: wave 1 of the frame builds the tap template first; the others come with the Band A and B templates.
- **Hint pictures of language skills.** A language skill has no hint family yet, so level 1 and 2 pulse the tiles (and play their sounds), as `tap_answer` does for a skill that no family knows.
- **"ё is never written е"** (frame F5): it needs a dictionary; the teacher's word lists will carry it.
- **The optical centring of a single letter in its tile** (fonts.md section 6): the design-director gives the numbers with the Band B letter templates; here a letter is centred by its line box.
