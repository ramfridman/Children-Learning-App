Device sizes are logical points: phone = iPhone 16 landscape 852×393 (safe areas 59 pt on each side, 21 pt at the bottom); smallest phone = iPhone SE 667×375; tablet = iPad 10th gen, 1180×820 landscape and 820×1180 portrait.
