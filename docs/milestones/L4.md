# L4: The Band A templates (LS08 listen and dialogic, LS11, LS12, LS10, LS03 drum, LS13)

**Status:** the owner approved the whole Languages frame and its milestone order on 2026-11-03 (`docs/languages/frame.md` section 8, section 6: "L4 Band A templates"), and has since asked to keep going. That approval covers this plan. Branch `languages-l4`, cut from `origin/main` (8ccdd59: L0 and L1 are merged).

This plan follows `docs/languages/frame.md` (section 2.3 for LS03, LS08, LS10, LS11, LS12 and LS13; section 2.5 for the build order; section 3 for placement, mastery and session shape; section 5.1 and 5.2 for speech and personal turns) and the three Band A batches (`docs/languages/{english,hebrew,russian}/band-a-content.md`), which are the real items these templates must play. Where the build differs from PLAN.md, PLAN.md is updated in the same commit.

## 1. Goal and "done when"

Every interaction that the three Band A batches ask for, apart from saying a word aloud (L3), can be authored, validated and played, on fixture packs and fake clips: a picture story with its pauses, a command the child carries out, a chant and a rhyme, a puppet's turn, the syllable drum, and the placement game. No content pack, no real voice, no recogniser.

The build order is that of frame section 2.5 for wave 1, minus LS09 (it needs the recognisers of L3): **LS08** (`listen`, `dialogic`) then **LS11**, **LS12**, **LS10**, **LS03** (`drum`), **LS13**. One commit for each, after its own tests and goldens. Everything that all of them share (the choice base, the choice row, the clip hook that can wait for a clip to end, the probe rules of the controller, the validator hooks) goes into the first commit, the one of LS08.

Every template has, without exception (frame section 2.3):
- the tap path (the microphone is never the only way, and in L4 there is no microphone at all);
- a three-step hint ladder whose level 2 **changes the representation**, and whose level 3 is "show me";
- a replay button, big targets, no timer, countdown or streak anywhere (the child-space source rule forbids the word, and a source test over every new file enforces it);
- at most 3 choices and one instruction at a time (Band A presentation);
- the course's direction and font (F1, F2), and two-clip lines (a Hebrew frame clip, then the target clip);
- every distractor tagged, with the 2-choice rule (frame section 2.3); free-response modes (the drum) have a classifier instead;
- nothing ever marked "wrong" for an unclear answer, and nothing stored or sent from a personal turn.

## 2. The decisions this plan makes

| D-4 | A **probe** (`purpose: probe`) has no lamp and no "show me", and its first answer ends it, right or wrong; the lesson says one neutral warm line (the placement document's `thanks`) and moves on. | D-5 | `ClipPlayer` returns `FutureOr<void>`: the lesson screen's player completes when the clips have played. Without a player (a test) the line waits a fixed pause instead. |
| D-6 | The child **turns the page** of a story with a big arrow; a page's narration plays when it comes up. | D-7 | Personal turns (LS10 `personal`) carry a closed **topic** (`name`, `age`, `likes`, `family`), a pass, no `correct` and no tag. ## 3. Acceptance criteria, per template

Each is reported one by one at the end, with its tests and its goldens (the four boards: phone, phone SE, tablet landscape, tablet portrait).

**LS08 `story`, modes `listen` and `dialogic`**
1. A book of 2 to 10 pages (a picture, one or two clips of narration, and optional hotspots that say a word when touched). A page's narration plays when it comes up, and the child turns the page; a replay says the page again. No page text on the screen (Band A: no reading).
2. `dialogic`: after the page named by `pauseAfter` the story stops and asks a question with 2 or 3 picture or text choices; the answer is scored like any choice (tags, hint ladder, one retry for two choices); the item's `feedback.correct` is the recast; the story then goes on to its last page.
3. Hint ladder: level 1 plays the page that answers it again; level 2 lays the pages so far in a row and plays them one by one (the representation changes: a sequence); level 3 shows the answer.
4. Validator: pages, hotspots (inside the picture), clips and pictures exist, `pauseAfter` is a page, `listen` carries no answer and is `purpose: practice`, every distractor of a `dialogic` is tagged.

**LS11 `listen_do`, modes `hotspots`, `buttons`, `drag`, `place`, `embodied`**
1. `hotspots`: the child touches one of 2 or 3 glowing parts of a picture (a body, a room); a part with no `at` does not exist. `buttons`: one of 2 or 3 action pictures; the friend's picture shows the chosen action. `drag`: one of 2 or 3 things is dragged to the target (a plate, a box, the friend), or touched and then the target touched. `place` (Hebrew): the one thing is dragged to one of 2 or 3 places. `embodied`: the child does it and touches "done"; unscored, never counted alone.
2. A wrong thing or place maps to the course's tag through the option's tag; the wrong thing goes back, never in red.
3. Ladder: level 1 lights the parts and says the command again; level 2 changes the representation (the friend does it on its own: the close-up or the action picture); level 3 shows the answer.

**LS12 `sing_along`, modes `chant` and `rhyme`**
1. `chant`: the lines play one after another; the pictures of a line light while it plays; a touch on a lit picture says its word; joining in is the child's own. It writes nothing. The chant opens and closes every visit (frame section 3.5), so it has no pause demand.
2. `rhyme`: the verse plays twice, then stops before its last word; 2 or 3 picture choices; the answer is scored; then the whole verse plays.
3. Ladder (rhyme): level 1 the last word of the first line again; level 2 the ends alone (the word pairs: the line-end word, then each option); level 3 the whole verse with the right picture lit.
4. No melody: the verses are spoken to a beat (the owner's OK is needed for any tune, so none is built).

**LS10 `puppet_talk`, modes `bubble` and `personal`**
1. `bubble`: the puppet speaks one turn (two clips); the child touches one of 2 or 3 answer bubbles that fit the scene (picture or text, each with a speaker that plays it without answering); the puppet answers (the item's `feedback.correct`). The say step (LS09) is L3.
2. `personal`: the topic is one of the four of frame section 5.2; the puppet goes first; the child touches a picture, a number card or a family picture, or the pass; the reply is generic and warm; the answer is never checked, kept, logged, synced or used. With the parent's switch off the puppet answers its own question and the lesson goes on. There is never an address or a follow-up.
3. Ladder: level 1 the puppet's line again, then each bubble slowly; level 2 the scene's key picture glows with the gloss; level 3 the puppet points at the right bubble.

**LS03 `sound_boxes`, mode `drum`**
1. The word is heard with its picture; the child taps a drum once for each part; a tap makes a beat sound and lights a dot (the count is the child's own, shown as dots, never as a number); a "that's all" button (the drum's own check mark) gives the count.
2. Classifier: the right count is right; one more and one fewer map to the course's tags (`one_more`, `one_fewer`; the Hebrew and Russian courses give the same tag to both); any other count is a retry with no tag and no evidence. The response is the count, `n=3`.
3. Ladder: level 1 the word slowly with a pause between beats; level 2 the beats clapped by hands, each lit (movement and light); level 3 the friend drums it and the count lights up.

**LS13 placement (`placement/v1`)**
1. A staircase for each strand of the course, exactly as frame section 3.2: two right answers go up a level and one wrong goes down; it stops after 2 reversals or 8 items for the strand; the placement is the highest level with at least 2/3 right, minus one unit; a strand whose first two probes fail at its floor stops at once; the questionnaire sets where a staircase starts and never where it ends; at most about 8 probe items in one visit, the strands in order (oral first); speech is never a probe; no result is shown to the child.
2. The strands and their levels are data (`<course>.placement`): items by id, the skills that a level assumes, the start by questionnaire answer, and a strand that is offered only on an answer (the English literacy strand).
3. Wired to L0: the lesson id is `placementIdFor(course)`, the answers replay into a per-strand entry point, `Engine.assume` takes the skills below it, and the parent's "we start here" gets its level.
4. Validator: probes are `purpose: probe`, of an allowed template (`tap_answer`, `sound_boxes`), never voiced, never unscored, never personal, all of the pack's own course.

## 5. Order of work and checks

After each template: `flutter analyze`, `dart format --set-exit-if-changed .`, its own tests, and its goldens looked at. At the end: the whole `flutter test` once. The gateway is not touched (no Deno change, no migration): the new templates are strings that `sync-push` already takes (`^[a-z_]{2,30}$`), and the responses are ids and numbers (`o2`, `n=3`).

## 6. Security items (for the security-engineer's review before merge)

1. **Rule 2, ids and enums only.** Every new field is pack content (signed). A child's touch is an option id, a count, or nothing; no text, no audio.
2. **Personal turns (frame section 5.2).** No attempt is written, nothing is logged, synced, shown again or used to choose content; the answer lives only in the session object and goes with it; the topic is a closed list; the parent's switch is honoured; a pass is always there. Tested by reading the attempts, the log and the engine after a personal turn.
3. **Rule 5.** All lines are authored; no model output reaches any new screen (the tutor is off for every language item, L0).
4. **Child safety.** No microphone, no recording, no timer or countdown anywhere in the new files; a chant never makes a demand; nothing says "wrong"; a probe says one neutral line whatever the answer.
5. **Packs.** The new checks only add refusals. The starter pack and every pack that passed before still pass. No new package, no new host.

## 7. What is deferred, and why

- **LS09 and every say step** (the say step of LS10, `echo` of LS08): L3 (the recognisers). Until then the child's own saying is not part of any L4 item.
- **Auto-advance of story pages and word timing** (`read_along` is wave 3): needs the voice director's timing (D-6).
- **LS08 `read_along`, `read_first`, `echo`;** LS12's melodies; LS03's boxes, colours and letters (Band B, L5).
- **The parent co-play card and the word cards at the end of a story:** they are pack data of the course milestones (ENG-1, HEB-1, RUS-1).
- **The course screens that start a placement visit or a lesson** (a language lesson on `LessonScreen` from the doors): the course home is still the L0 placeholder; the placement game runs on the lesson screen when it is given its flow, and the course milestones connect it to the home.
- **The "balanced pair" served together** (the check of a binary skill) and the session builder's use of `purpose: probe`: the validator checks them; the builder serving them is the course milestones'.
- **The questionnaire screens** for the answers that set a staircase's start (the screens are L0's parent pages; the staircase takes the answers as a map).
- **Real pictures of the stories, scenes and drums** (the design-director's): the goldens use the approved pictures of `content/languages/pictures` and fixture shapes.

## 9. Built (2026-11-03)

All six templates are built, each with its tests and goldens, in the order of frame section 2.5 (LS09 waits for L3), one commit each. A summary of what was built differently from, or beyond, the plan above:

- **Shared base** (in the LS08 commit): `ChoiceActivity`, `ChoiceSession`, `ChoiceRow`, `withTallStage`, `completePractice`, `askedLine`, `replayQuiet`, `ItemFlow`, `noHelp` and the hint pictures of all five templates. The voice director got `untilQuiet()` (LS12 commit), because a chant needs the end of a line and `sayClips` returns at the start.
- **`ActivityFrame.showHelp`** (LS13): a probe has no lamp and no "show me" at all, not dimmed ones.
- **A probe looks the same for any answer** (found by the golden of LS13): the tile of a settled answer is only chosen, the same neutral ring for a right and a wrong one, in `tap_answer`, in the choice sessions and in the drum; the companion and the line are the same too.
- **Personal turns** write no attempt at all (decision D-3), proved against the real database, the outbox, the alerts and the log (`ls10_personal_safety_test.dart`).
- **Pictures for the goldens** are the approved ones of `content/languages/pictures`; the scene, hotspot and drum pictures are fixture compositions, not the design-director's.

### Deferred, in addition to section 7
- **The parent's "we start here: level N"** (`StrandProgress.startLevel`) is not wired: the parent's progress strands (`lis`, `spk`, `pa`, ...) are not the placement strands (`o`, `l`, `1` to `5`); the engine does get the assumed skills. The course milestones decide how the two are shown together.
- **A provider-level test of the placement wiring** (`languageCoursesProvider` with a real database): the staircase, the replay, `assumedSkills` and `Engine.assume` are tested one by one; the single line that joins them in the provider is not.
- **The phone SE stage at a question** (a story's pause, a puppet's bubbles with speakers) is small (the tiles and speakers take the room), and the personal cards scroll when more than a few do not fit; the English family turn has 10 cards in the batch and the template takes 8 (a content question for the English teacher: two turns, or fewer cards).
- **Auto-advance of story pages** (D-6), **word timing** and the **melodies**.
- **`purpose: check` for a binary pair served together** is still the course milestones' (the validator checks the pair).
