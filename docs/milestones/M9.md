## 1. Goal and "done when"

The app stops being a prototype with five skills and becomes something the children can use every day: enough lessons to last months, in all six lands, in the voices of their friends, with real art, on the family's phones and the iPad.

PLAN's acceptance for M9: **every activity passes the headless playthrough, and the release gate checklist is complete.** In plain words, the criteria I will report against:

1. **About 95 skills** (Bands A and B of Numbers, Colours, Shapes, Logic and Computer science, plus the core of Money and Maths) with about 300 activities and about 2,400 items, all through the validator.
2. **12 templates** playable by tap, drag and (where it makes sense) voice. Today there are 3.
3. **Every activity passes a headless playthrough**: a simulated child plays each activity in all its variants (right answers, each wrong answer, every hint level, "show me") and nothing crashes, dead-ends or leaves the child without a next step.
4. **Hebrew reviewed**: every child-facing string and line has `review: approved` from a person (or is listed as open in the report).
5. **Voices**: every line of the release has a recording for every character that is shipped with that voice (Q5).
6. **Art**: every skill, land, sticker and reward has its picture (real art, or a listed placeholder).
7. **Release gate checklist** (section 6) complete, and the app installed and working on the owner's Samsung phone, his wife's phone and the iPad.

## 2. How we verify

As before: everything that can be proved without a child is proved by tests first (the validator over every file, the simulator, the headless playthrough, goldens in right to left with the real fonts, the Deno and pgTAP suites), then phone checks by the owner, then **child tests** (sections 4 and 14 of the curriculum): a template passes when at least 80 percent of children start correctly after the spoken instruction alone. The honest limits, which the report will say plainly: speech recognition for children is still measured only on adults (M5), the efficacy of the lessons is not measured by this milestone, and the child tests are the owner's own two children.

## 15. More activities per skill, and the shape builder (2026-10-28, branch `m9-content`)

The owner asked for two things: more activities for every skill, and the shape builder.

**More activities.** Every skill now has **at least six activities** (SHP-A-06, the new one, has five), 316 in all in **72 lessons** (the pack is **0.6.0**, published to the server). Most of them are written by new kinds in `tools/author.py` that build the question, the traps and the help from a few facts: `cfind` and `cmatch` (a thing and a colour), `pat` (what comes next, what is missing, is it a pattern), `odd` and `same`, `story` (first, middle, last), `size3` (three sizes in a row), `pair`, `shape` (shapes in colours), `madd` and `mtake` (add one more, take one away), `shop`; plus new parameters for the older kinds. Each new kind knows the Hebrew definite forms and the gender of its nouns, so a colour, a thing or a number is always written in agreement. About 120 new pictures (shapes in five colours, things in three sizes, stories, pairs, lines) were drawn; they are placeholders like the others. The new activities sit in 30 new lessons ("more of ...") so a child who finishes a lesson meets new questions, not the same ones, and the path (`sequence`) offers them in order.

**The shape builder (T10, `shape_builder` 1.0).** A picture made of shapes (a house, a tree, a face, a train, a rocket) is shown as grey silhouettes; the shapes wait in the tray below, with a decoy or two; the child touches a shape and then its place, or drags it there. It snaps in, turned the way the picture needs it, so a child never has to turn anything (the rocket's body and fins are turned). A shape in a wrong place is a "not yet" and goes back; after three, only the right place takes it and the help points at it. A place answers only where its shape is, not on the empty corners of its square, so a door on a house body does not block the body. The author gives each shape as a centre, a size and a turn, and the tool fits the picture to the smallest frame. This unlocks SHP-A-06 (the outline puzzle).

## 16. Band B and the last three templates (2026-10-28, branch `m9-content`)

**New templates (all `1.0`):**
- **T09 `number_line`**: a line from the small number on the left to the large on the right (never mirrored). `place`: touch the tick where the marker belongs. `jump`: the marker stands on a number and hops; touch where it lands. A touch is the answer. The mirror of the right tick (the line read from the right) carries its own tag, `M-NL-DIR`. After three mistakes only the right tick is taken and the guide counts along the line; a new touch ends the guide's demonstration at once.
- **T08 `balance`**: a scale with two pans. `compare`: which pan is heavier, or the same (tiles drawn left, same, right). `balance`: add blocks to the lighter pan until the scale is level. When a sum is being compared (`hideTilt`) the beam stays level until the child has answered, so the lean does not give the answer away.
- **T21 `spot_error`**: a row made by a friend with one mistake in it (pictures, numbers, arrows, signs); touch the mistake. After three wrong touches only the mistake is taken, and the help rings it.
- `tap_answer` rows can now also show numbers and signs (`n:7`, `op:+`), so a number sentence such as `3 + 4 = ?` is drawn on the stage.
