כֹּחַ־עַל = דֶּרֶךְ לְהָבִין
  • Original kid heroes; powers are learning representations used by "try another way".
  • No spiders/webs, no armour suits, no green giants, no ice sisters, no alien pets.
  • Palettes avoid red/gold, red/blue, green/purple hero schemes; pass the safe-colour lint.
  • Shared squad badge: spark in a hexagon. Cape optional, soft, with a spark pocket.
