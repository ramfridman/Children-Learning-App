# M8: Rewards and the world (growth, stickers, the Trophy Hall, the ceremony, parent ribbons)

**Status:** Plan written on 2026-10-28, **waiting for the owner's OK**. M7 is merged. Nothing of M8 is coded. The work will go on a new branch, `m8-rewards-world`.

This plan follows PLAN.md (World, characters & story › How the world rewards learning; Mastery with memory; Awards, prizes & the Trophy Hall; Sync rules › rewards; Parent controls › Rewards) and the build-plan row for M8. Where it differs, PLAN.md is updated in the same commit, as always.

## 1. Goal and "done when"

What a child understands becomes something they can see and keep: a world that grows, stickers for finishing a lesson, and trophies that come only from understanding that lasted. A parent can attach a real promise to a trophy and is told when it is earned.

PLAN's acceptance for M8, in plain words:

1. **Trophies come only from lasting-mastery events.** Nothing that is only speed, time played, number of tries or a fresh (not yet lasting) mastery can earn a trophy.
2. **A parent ribbon shows on the right trophy:** the right child, the right land and tier, and the parent is told when it is earned.

Plus the criteria I will report against (each is proved by a test or a phone check, section 5):

3. **Nothing is ever taken away.** A replay, a late event from another phone, or a reset of the engine never removes a sticker, a trophy, a star or a prize.
4. **The world shows the truth.** Each skill's object is a pencil sketch while the child practises, in full colour at fresh mastery, and has fruit and a golden star at lasting mastery. A skill that needs a revisit shows a leaf that needs watering and a bell flag. Nothing is padlocked and nothing breaks.
5. **Stickers:** one for each finished lesson, never random, never lost.
6. **The Trophy Hall and the ceremony:** shelves by land, locked trophies as soft silhouettes that say how to earn them, a golden-star counter, and a ceremony the companion presents with its own voice. The ceremony is short, can be skipped by a tap, respects "reduce motion", and a parent can turn it off.
7. **Healthy by design (CLAUDE.md):** no loot boxes, no streaks, no timers, no guilt, no prize for speed or for playing longer. A test proves that the reward rules cannot see durations or answer speed.
8. **It works with no network, with the AI off and with voice off,** and it adds no new network host or package.

## 2. How we verify

As before: everything that can be proved without a phone or a vendor is proved by tests first (Dart unit and widget tests with golden screenshots in right-to-left with the real fonts, the Deno suite, pgTAP). Then phone checks on your Samsung. The one honest limit: **the starter pack has only 2 skills (one in Numbers, one in Colours)**, so no real land can reach a silver or gold trophy until M9 brings about 95 skills. The rules are therefore proved on synthetic skill graphs, and the phone check uses a dev-flavour demo land (Q3).

## 4. Tasks, in order

### Part 8a: earning and the world

**A. Reward rules and storage**
- A1. A pure `RewardRules` that takes the replayed engine state (never durations, speeds or counts of plays) and the installed skills, and returns the awards that are due: stickers, golden stars, land trophies, medals, decorations. Defined once, tested alone, importing no UI code (as the engine).
- A2. Drift `rewards` table (database version 8): child, award key (for example `trophy:counting:silver`, `star:NUM-A-01`, `sticker:lesson.butterflies:1`), when, and what it came from. A unique key per child makes a grant happen once. Nothing ever deletes a row except removing the child.
- A3. `RewardService`: after a visit ends and when the app starts it asks the rules what is due, writes the missing rows, and returns the new ones for the celebration. The one-time catch-up of Q8.
- A4. Rewards sync as their own events (`type = 'reward'`), so a second phone shows the same stickers and trophies, applies them without ever removing one, and replay cannot undo them.

**B. Stickers and Sticker Island**
- B1. One sticker for each finished lesson, chosen by a fixed rule from the lesson (not random). It is revealed in the lesson's celebration, the medium tier of PLAN.
- B2. Sticker Island: a screen of the child's stickers (the island sign on the map opens it).

**C. World growth**
- C1. `worldObject` on skills (Q10) and a small catalog of objects. One drawing per object, shown in stages by the code: sketch (pale, outline), full colour, colour with fruit and a golden star. A "leaf that needs watering" and a bell flag for a skill that needs a revisit. Nothing is ever removed.
- C2. The **land view**: tapping a land with content shows its island with the objects growing and a way into the existing lesson. (The 5 to 8 stepping-stone path comes with the content of M9.)
- C3. The bell flag opens a **revisit mission** of 3 to 5 items in a fresh context, built by the existing session builder with only the revisit phase for that skill. Passing a check a week or more after mastery is what the engine already calls lasting understanding; the golden star then appears.
- C4. The golden-star count, shown on the map and later in the Hall.

### Part 8b: showing and giving

**D. The Trophy Hall and the ceremony**
- D1. The Hall: shelves by land, bronze to gold, locked trophies as soft silhouettes with how to earn them and progress, the golden-star counter, medals and prizes. Tapping a trophy replays its moment: the `childLine` of what was learned and an icon.
- D2. The ceremony: lights dim, a spotlight, the trophy spins in, confetti, the companion's own line (voice or caption). Drawn in code with `CustomPainter`; with "reduce motion" it becomes a still spotlight and a fade. A tap skips it after a moment. It never traps a child and shows no timer.
- D3. A queue of at most one ceremony per lesson end; when several tiers unlock together only the highest is celebrated (Q8).

**E. Prizes, medals, decorations**
- E1. Accessory colour sets applied by the character renderer; the child chooses what the companion wears (synced as an id on the child profile).
- E2. Habit medals (Q6) detected from the attempts the engine already has.
- E3. Three island decorations at 10, 20 and 30 skills understood.

**F. Parent ribbons and controls**
- F1. A parent screen "Prizes": every trophy of each child with a field for the promise (Q9). The text is stored in the family settings (whitelist extended) and shown as a ribbon on that child's trophy.
- F2. When a trophy with a ribbon is earned, the child's device raises an alert of a new info kind, `ribbonEarned`, carrying only the child and the trophy key. The push says only "יש התראה חדשה"; the parent's alert screen looks up the promise.
- F3. Parent switch: ceremonies on or off. When off, a new trophy goes quietly to the Hall.

**G. Server (small)**
- G1. Migration: allow `reward` events (payload: a kind from a fixed list, the award key, nothing else), the child profile's `outfit_id`, and the new alert kind. pgTAP extended. Functions `sync-push`, `sync-pull`, `child-put`, `settings-put` and `alert-create` get the new strict shapes; deleting a child already removes its events.

### Phase H: proof and report
- H1. Tests (section 5), phone checks, all six CI jobs green.
- H2. Report in the standard form, saying plainly what was not measured on real content.

## 7. Security and child-safety controls this milestone touches

| Control | How M8 keeps it |
|---|---|
| Devices send ids, numbers and enums only | Reward events carry a kind and an award key; the only free text is the parent's promise, bounded and kept in the family settings (Q9), never in an event, alert, push, log or prompt |
| Four checks on every function | The changed functions keep token, signed request, device active and in family, and role (a child device may send reward events and alerts; only a parent may write settings) |
| `app` schema unexposed | Migration follows the same grants; pgTAP extended |
| No ads, purchases, timers, streaks or guilt in the child's space | No purchasable or random reward, no streak, no visible timer; rewards never depend on speed or time played; locked trophies are calm silhouettes, not a reproach |
| No child audio or data to a vendor | The ceremony lines contain no child name; paid generation only after count and cost are shown and you type your OK |
| Local data encrypted | The `rewards` table is in the same encrypted database |
| Hebrew | RTL, Heebo and Secular One only, line height 1.7 for nikud text; all new strings are marked `needs-editor`, and no nikud is generated at runtime |

## 9. Risks and unknowns

- **No real land can earn a silver or gold trophy until M9** (Q3). The machinery is proved on synthetic content and the dev demo land; the first real trophy will appear only when a land has at least 3 skills understood for a week.
- **Placeholder art.** It will look like a prototype. The ids make it replaceable, and the report will say which pieces are placeholders.
- **A reward is a promise.** If a trophy were ever granted by mistake it could not be taken back (criterion 3). The rules are therefore tested hardest of all, and the catch-up (Q8) is a dry run first: it prints what it would grant before it writes anything in the dev flavour.
- **Ceremonies can annoy.** One per lesson end, skippable, off by switch, quiet with reduce motion.
- **The parent's promise on the server** (Q9) is a new kind of free text; it is bounded, filtered like the nickname and removed with the child, and it counts in the privacy text for M9.
- **Sync of rewards while two phones play.** The unique award key makes a double grant impossible; a test covers both orders.
