# B-26 Nitzi's home: references for the drawings (design-director, 2026-10-29)

The owner's rule (2026-10-28): before drawing something real, look at real photographs and read the facts, then draw our
own original in plain SVG shapes. Nothing below was traced, copied or downloaded; the photographs were looked at in the
browser (Wikimedia Commons media search and Wikipedia), and only what makes each thing recognisable was taken. The
drawings are in `tools/make_nitzi_home.py` (the same sources are in its header).
