## 0. The plan in one page

1. **Band C is the content of late grade 1 to grade 3**: numbers to 100, then 1,000 and 10,000; addition and subtraction within 100 and beyond; multiplication and division; measuring, time, money and data; angles, solids and moves; logic puzzles; programs with loops. A child reaches it by mastery, never by age. A child aged 6;6 and up sees it in the older look (presentation stage P4, B-29).
2. **73 skills** (curriculum §4), in **9 batches**. Batches C1 to C8 follow the Ministry's new programme for grades 1 to 3. Batch C9 holds what that programme does not teach in grades 1 to 3 (fractions, grams and kilograms, if-then-else, variables); the owner decides on it after C8.
3. **Band B is not complete.** The pack has 49 of 79 Band B skills and 51 of 56 Band A skills. **24 unbuilt A and B skills sit under Band C skills as prerequisites.** M10 builds each of them in the batch that first needs it (the "bridge"). It never rewires a prerequisite around a missing skill.
4. **Templates:** 4 new templates (equal groups and arrays, measure it, a place-value mat, a grid), 1 new answer type (type the number on a keypad), and new modes in 8 existing templates. The biggest job is the robot (turns, walls, repeat, predict, debug).
5. **Two app fixes come first**, or Band C would harm younger children: the lesson path must skip a lesson whose skills are not unlocked (audit F6), and the warm-up must not trust one lucky answer (F7). I recommend the language courses' staircase (LS13, `engine/staircase.dart`) for the lands' warm-up too.
6. **About 1,300 new activities** (Band C and the bridge), about 230 lessons, about 1,600 new Hebrew lines (text only), 32 misconception records from the catalogue plus 16 new tags.
7. **B-36 stays its own pack and goes first.** M10 needs only its catalogue renames (M-NOT, M-PPW) and its tag rule. See §9.

## 1. Goal and "done when"

A child who has mastered Band B in a land finds new lessons there, in an older look, that teach the grade 2 and grade 3 core correctly, diagnose the classic errors (smaller-from-larger, face value, adding instead of multiplying, the hands of the clock) and repair them with a different picture.

Acceptance criteria, reported one by one at the end of each batch and of M10:

1. **Skills and graph.** Every skill of the batch is in the pack with the prerequisites of curriculum §4 (or of an approved change in §8). Every prerequisite exists in the pack. The graph has no cycle (validator).
2. **Enough to reach mastery.** Every skill has at least 12 activities (core skills 16, bridge skills 10), at least 2 representations (core 3, §5.5), at least 1 understanding check (core 2 of different types, §5.6), and items from at least 2 `variantGroup`s for the delayed check. `app/test/mastery_reachable_test.dart` passes for every new skill.
3. **Tagged wrong answers.** Every item with 3 or more options has two distractors with distinct tags (or the same tag when the skill watches one tag, the E1 rule of B-15). Two-option items are at most half of a skill's items and never a check on their own. Every number-entry item lists its expected wrong values with tags. Every watch tag has a dedicated diagnostic item, and also an item where the misconception gives the right answer by luck (§5.3).
4. **Machine-checked.** The maths teacher's checker recomputes every answer from the item's own numbers: 0 wrong answers, 0 distractors that are also right, and every tag rule reproduces its distractor (for example `smallbig(52, 27) = 35`). The CS teacher's simulator runs every robot task: each is solvable within its step limit, the shortest solution is accepted where "shortest" is asked, and every distractor program fails in a way the child can see. Both report 0 failures.
5. **Hints.** Every activity has a picture at hint levels 1, 2 and 3 (the B-40 test). Level 2 changes the representation. Nothing marks the answer before level 3 (the B-40 Appendix B lint finds 0).
6. **Left to right.** Number lines, columns, rows, hundred charts, clocks, robot arrows and grid coordinates are drawn left to right in an LTR isolate and never mirror. `<` and `>` appear only inside LTR maths tokens. Goldens on the four boards (phone, phone SE, tablet landscape, tablet portrait), looked at as pictures.
7. **Band rules.** Up to 5 choices for Band C, 8 to 12 items per lesson, pointed instructions, no timer anywhere; fluency is measured silently and never shown.
8. **The path.** A child who has not unlocked a Band C skill is never offered its lesson (a path test with a 4-year-old profile). A child who knows Band B reaches Band C on a land within the first warm-up (a staircase test).
9. **Hebrew.** Every new string is `"review": "needs-editor"` until the hebrew-text-editor makes it `text-ready`. Maths words follow CLAUDE.md (÷ by default, `:` selectable; = read שָׁוֶה; × read כָּפוּל). No voice is generated. The batch's new lines are listed for the voicing (B-45).
10. **Green and signed.** Analyzer, format and the whole suite green after every step; the qa-engineer verified the batch once; the owner signed the pack; PLAN.md updated in the same commit as any difference.

**What the new programme changes in curriculum §3.2** (it was written from the older milestone files):
- **Letter values (NUM-C-05) are grade 3, א to ת,** not grade 1 to ל. PLAN.md line 128 says "grade 1–2"; it should change.
- **No fractions topic in grades 1 to 3.** Grade 3 only prepares for fractions. No weight topic either (I searched the extracted text of all three files for both).
- **Time:** grade 2 is whole and half hours (with digital); grade 3 is minutes. Quarter hours are not named.
- **The multiplication table:** grade 2 is ×2, ×4, ×5 and ×10; grade 3 is the whole table. The old row "to 6 × 6" is out of date.
- **Whole tens to 100 (MATH-C-03) and simple maps are grade 1.** Right angles and perimeter are grade 2; area and kinds of angle are grade 3.

These are in the syllabus proposals of §8.

## 9. B-36: fold it in or not?

**Recommendation: do not fold B-36 into M10. Finish it first, as its own signed pack.**

- B-36 repairs content that already exists (second representations for 75 skills, tags for the 57 untagged items, NUM-B-16, MATH-B-06 and B-07, 1,142 hint lines). It is half built on branch `next-pack`. Mixing it with 1,300 new activities would make one review the owner cannot do in one sitting.

## 11. Presentation, reading and voice in Band C

- **Look:** presentation stage P4 (6;6 and up, B-29): grown-up look, never babyish, even for Band A content.
- **Reading:** pointed instructions on screen for every prompt; the parent's reading setting (B-29) can turn them into captions after the voice once voices exist.
- **Voice:** text only now (owner, 2026-11-06). New lines carry `vo:` ids and wait for B-45, as the 712 Band B lines do today; the screen text must stand alone. `voiceAnswer` is false on new items; the voice-and-sound-director and the recogniser data decide later which become spoken (numbers above 20 are not measured).
- **Words:** formal terms come in Band C (מחובר, הפרש, מכפלה, עשרות, יחידות, מאות, זווית ישרה, היקף, שטח, פאה, מקצוע, קודקוד, הזזה, שיקוף, סיבוב, עודף, אגורות). The editor adds nikud and checks them; how ÷ is read aloud (I expect "חלקי") is the editor's question for the owner.
