## 0. The plan in one page

1. **Band B is where letters start.** In all three courses a child meets letters taught whole (shape, sound, name and writing together, D16), hears and splits the sounds of words, reads the first words and builds them from tiles. English and Russian also grow their spoken vocabulary; Hebrew grows its oral language and stories.
2. **Five new or extended templates** (frame wave 2): **LS02** write a letter (trace, copy, memory, on the F6 stroke data), **LS03** sound boxes in full, **LS05** change one part, **LS04** blend slide, **LS06** build a word. Plus two pieces the batches need and nobody has built: the **rest of LS01** (text faces in sort, match, spot and order; tap-all; text stem; cloze; sequence) and two **LS08** modes (`follow`, `echo`).
3. **Three content batches**, each in sub-batches: about 355 English items, 520 Hebrew and 400 Russian (targets; the teachers set the real numbers).
4. **Voices come later** (owner, 2026-11-06). Every spoken line is written now as text with a clip id and a pronunciation row, listed as pending in the pack. Nothing is voiced and nothing is paid. Every template plays with every clip pending, as the app does today.
5. **Do first** (marked **First** below): the rest of LS01 (it also unblocks the Hebrew Band A batch), then LS02 with one real letter group per course as a pilot slice, then the other templates in the frame's order.
6. **Eight questions for the owner** (§13). The most important: how a writing skill is mastered (Q1), and whether Band B reaches the children before its sounds are voiced (Q7).

## 1. Goal and "done when"

A child with Band B content open in any course can be introduced to a letter, trace it, copy it and write it from memory; split a heard word into sounds or syllables with tokens; change one part of a syllable and hear the change; slide through a written word and hear it blend; build a heard word from tiles and hear "every sound is there" for a sound-right spelling; read a first word and pick its picture. Every item of the three Band B batches can be authored, validated and played on fixture packs and on the real batch packs, with every clip pending.

"Done" means: every acceptance criterion of §10 reported one by one, each template verified once by the qa-engineer, the security items of §11 reviewed, and each content sub-batch validated and played through once.

## 5. The template specs (in brief)

Every template keeps the rules of frame §2.3 without exception: the tap path always works; a three-step hint ladder whose **level 2 changes the representation** and whose level 3 is "show me"; a replay button; big targets; no timer; every distractor tagged with a tag of its own course (never an accent tag), with the 2-choice rule; the course's direction (F1) and font (F2); two-clip lines (a Hebrew frame clip, then the target clip). Free-response modes (LS02, LS03, LS06) have classifiers instead of fixed distractors: an error they can name maps to a tag, an error they cannot name is a retry with no tag and no evidence. Response codes are ids and numbers only (CLAUDE.md rule 2).

### 5.1 LS01, the rest (**First**)

- **What it adds:** text faces (a letter, a CV unit, a syllable, a word) on sort cards and bin labels, on match faces, on `spot_error` cells (`txt:`) and on order tiles; sound-only cards in a sort; **tap-all** (several right answers in one row, each tapped; the item is right when all right ones and no wrong one are tapped); **(d) text stem** (a written word or sentence on the stage, picture options; "read it to me" is hint level 1 and lowers the credit to 0.8); **(e) cloze** (a sentence with a gap and 3 word tiles); **(f) sequence** (picture or text tiles into slots in the course's direction).
- **Schema shape:** the existing option fields of L1 (`text`, `clip`, `answer.face`) on every LS01 template; `scene.stem` (`text`, `clip` for "read it to me"); `answer.correct` becomes a list for tap-all (`answer.all: true`); `scene.cloze` (`text` with one `_` gap); sequence uses `build_pattern` slots with `answer.order`.
- **Hint ladder:** as each template today, with text-stem items getting level 1 "read it to me" (the stem's clip). For Hebrew decoding items, level 2 is the LS05 wheel and level 3 the LS04 `units` demonstration (§5.9).
- **Validator:** the F5 checks of L1 on every new face (script, decodable words for a `letters` skill, Hebrew pointed in Bands A and B); tap-all has at least 1 right and 2 tagged wrong cards, at most 6 cards under Band B presentation; a cloze has one gap; a sequence's order is complete and in the course's direction.
- **Decided 2026-11-06 (the English teacher's pilot points; these touch L5-1 as it is built):**
  - **An option with no clip.** In an item whose options play their own sound (LS01 (b)), a wrong option may have no clip of its own: the look-alike tile of a letter not yet taught (eng f l o d q b h m j, rus Л Д С Ч Н И). Such a tile shows no speaker and plays nothing when tapped before the answer. Its first-error feedback skips "that is …" + the option's word (there is no word to say) and plays the tag's line instead (eng `he-fb-look-alike`), then the target again. In such an item the validator allows an option without a clip only when it is a distractor (never the answer) and its tag is of family SHAPE. Items whose options never sound (a written word to read, heb B-19, B-20.7) are unchanged. Reason: the child must not hear an untaught letter's name or sound by accident (frame §5.3), and a feedback line must never point at a sound that is not there.
  - **An untaught look-alike letter as a wrong tile.** In a letter-knowledge skill, a single-letter face that is a SHAPE-tagged distractor may use a letter of a later group: the F5 decodable check is for words and units, not for a lone letter tile, and the tile is never the answer, never read and never voiced. The course may forbid it for itself (the Hebrew S1 slice shows no letter of a later group, its own binding rule).
  - **Text faces on LS11 `place` targets** (eng B-16 i16, the letter stones): **in L5-1**. The target face reuses LS01's text face (a), at the band's reading size. It is the embodied item of a letter skill (the child moves the friend to the letter), worth the small cost; if it slips, the item becomes an LS01 tap and the skill keeps its symbolic and pictorial items.

### 5.2 LS02 write a letter: `trace` 2.0 (**First**)

- **What the child does.** `intro` (unscored, the worked example of a bundle, frame §5.3): the letter big; its sound in a key word with a picture (a stop heard inside a CV unit in Hebrew, in a syllable in Russian, clipped in English); its name as a separate fact ("its name is …, it says …"); the dot walks the path; then one trace. `trace`: the dotted path with the full guide or the start dot. `copy`: the model beside an empty box (`model: beside`), or shown and then covered (`model: hide`, heb B-26). `memory`: no model; the voice says the sound or the name (`cue: sound | name`). A `word` canvas is a row of letter boxes in the course's direction (heb B-27 own name in Band B; short words in Band C).
- **Schema shape:**
  ```
  { "type": "trace", "templateVersion": "2.0",
    "mode": "intro | trace | copy | memory",
    "canvas": "letter | word",
    "forms": ["latin.s"],            // 1 form, or 2 to 6 for a word canvas; from the pack's stroke set of its course
    "source": "forms | own_name",    // own_name: the letters of the child's name, on the device (heb B-27)
    "guide": "full | start",         // trace only
    "model": "beside | hide",        // copy only
    "cue": "sound | name",           // memory only
    "prompt": { "vo": "vo:…", "target": "vo:…" },
    "classify": { "start": tag, "direction": tag, "lines": tag, "mirror": tag, "partners": { "latin.d": tag } } }
  ```
  The stroke set (`content/languages/strokes/<set>.json`, format `nitznutz.strokes/1`) travels inside the course's signed pack. The 1-to-3-stroke limit of today's `trace` becomes 1 to 6 for a form id (Ё has 6).
- **The free-stroke check** (copy and memory; trace keeps today's path check): at the **end of each stroke**, never during it (Patchan and Puranik 2016). The child's stroke is resampled and compared with the nearest model stroke not yet matched: the start within a radius of the model's start; the first move within an angle of the model's first `moves` entry; the path within a band of the model polyline, on the guide lines (positions are in the 100 by 100 box, so a letter on the wrong lines is seen). A `dot` is a tap near its point; a `short` mark is checked by start and direction only. Each tolerance is a named constant that the qa-engineer tunes on adult and child-like samples. **Mirror and partner:** if the whole letter fits its mirror image, or a listed look-alike form (b/d, ш/щ, ב/כ), better than the target by a margin, that classifier fires.
- **Stroke order is not scored** (see Q2): a stroke matched out of order still counts; after a success the model replays once in its order. Reason: start and direction carry legibility; a fixed order did not help, and for children under 4½ hindered, symbol learning (Merritt et al. 2020); the English and Russian stroke models also say direction matters more than order.
- **Classifiers to tags:** start or direction wrong → the course's STROKE tag (`M-ENG-STROKE-RTL`, `M-ENG-STROKE-CW`, `M-HEB-STROKE`, `M-RUS-STROKE`, adopted 2026-11-06, frame §4.6); off the lines → `M-ENG-LINES`; mirror → `M-HEB-MIRROR`, `M-ENG-BD`; partner → the pair tag (`M-RUS-SHAPE`, `M-HEB-SHAPE-*`, `M-ENG-BD`). Anything else is a retry with no tag.
- **Hint ladder:** level 1 the start dot and the arrow of the stroke the child is on; level 2 the stroke drawn slowly once, then the box clears (a new representation: motion); level 3 trace mode for this item, the same in all three courses (writing-mastery §7.1). A stroke that misses fades softly and the start dot returns; no red, no buzzer.
- **Response code:** `ok`, or the stroke number and the classifier (`s2.dir`). The child's points live only in the session and are dropped with it. Own-name items write `built` or nothing.
- **Validator:** every form exists in the stroke set of the item's course (latin for eng, cyrillic for rus, hebrew-print in Band B; hebrew-script only in skills flagged `script`, L6); a stroke set marked `needs-teacher` is refused in a language pack (all four sets are `teacher-checked` today); `intro` is unscored; `memory` has its cue clip; the classify tags are in the course's catalogue, of family STROKE or SHAPE; a word canvas has at most 6 boxes (two rows for a longer own name, the design-director's call).
- **Also in this step:** the A-14 scene background and the mover sprite of `strokes.md` §4 item 7. The Hebrew Band A batch needs them and they are not built.

### 5.3 LS03 sound boxes: `sound_boxes` 1.1

- **What the child does.** Hears a word with its picture and drags one token per unit into boxes in the course's direction. Then, by the item's steps: `colour` (rus: red vowel, blue hard, green soft), `stress` (rus B-17: mark the stressed vowel), `which` ("where is the sound /m/?": tap a box). `drum` stays as built.
- **Schema shape:** `mode: drum | boxes`; `unit: syllable | cv | phoneme`; `word` (`sprite`, `clip`, `slow`, `units`: each with a `clip` and a `kind`: `vowel`, `hard`, `soft`, `cv`, `c`); `steps` (an ordered subset of `count`, `colour`, `stress`, `which`); `answer` (`count`, `colours` as a string such as `brb`, `stress`, `which`); `classify` (named classifiers to the course's tags).
- **Classifiers:** one more or one fewer (`M-ENG-SYLL`, `M-ENG-WEAK-SYLL`, `M-HEB-SYLL`, `M-RUS-SYLL`); one token a syllable (`M-ENG-SEG-SYLL`); a cluster short (`M-ENG-CLUSTER`); a CV for a C (`M-HEB-CV-FOR-C`); soft for hard or hard for soft (`M-RUS-HARDSOFT`); the last syllable as stressed (`M-RUS-STRESS`); the end for the start in `which` (`M-RUS-NOTFIRST`); a vowel coloured as a consonant (no tag exists: §14 item 3). Off by two or more: a retry, no tag.
- **Response code:** `n=3.c=brb.s=1.w=2` (numbers and three colour letters; never a word).
- **Hint ladder:** level 1 the word slowly, units stretched with a pause between them; level 2 a new representation per step: for the count step, by unit (decided 2026-11-06, the English teacher's point): **syllable** and **cv** units, hands clap each unit as it lights; **phoneme** units, the mouth picture of each sound as its token lights (a clap per sound in a one-syllable word would model the syllable split, `M-ENG-SEG-SYLL`; articulation pictures help beginners segment phonemes, Castiglioni-Spalten and Ehri 2003, Boyer and Ehri 2011); the "can you sing it?" test with the mouth picture (colour: a vowel can be sung; smile for soft), the friend calls the word from far away (stress); level 3 the friend lays the tokens, then the child does it.
- **Validator:** `units` length equals `answer.count`; `colours` only in `rus` and as long as the count; the stress step only in a skill flagged `stress`, with marks shown only where the route allows (D15); `which` within the count; every tag in the catalogue.

### 5.4 LS05 change one part: `change_part` 1.0

- **What the child does.** A frame with one changeable place (two for the Hebrew wheel: the letter and the sign). `explore` (unscored teaching screen): turn the wheel or slide the strip and hear each new unit or word. `make` (scored): "make the one you hear".
- **Per course:** heb the CV wheel (*גלגל סימני ניקוד*), the teaching screen of each step S1 to S8 and the level-2 hint of every decoding item; rus the vowel strip in pairs а–я, о–ё, у–ю, ы–и, э–е (B-21; the level-2 re-teach of `M-RUS-HARDSOFT`, `-YI`, `-YOT`); eng the vowel swap and "add the e" (optional, Band C).
- **Schema shape:** `mode: explore | make`; `frame` (`text`, `slots` of 1 or 2 places); `parts` (each `id`, `text`, `result` with `text` and `clip`, and `tag` when it is a distractor of `make`); `answer.parts`.
- **Classifiers:** the distractor part's tag (`M-HEB-VOWEL-SKIP`, `-VOWEL-OU`, `-VOWEL-SHAPE`, `-DOT`; `M-RUS-HARDSOFT`, `-YI`, `-YOT`; `M-ENG-AE-E`, `-E-SILENT`).
- **Response code:** the part ids (`p1`, `p1.p4`).
- **Hint ladder (make):** level 1 the target again, then the child's current result; level 2 the key-word picture whose first unit it is (heb) or the mouth picture (rus soft: a smile); level 3 the friend turns to it.
- **Validator:** every result is a CV unit or a word from the teacher's list (heb with the editor's nikud), decodable at the skill; every result has a clip id; `make` offers at most 4 parts under Band B presentation (`explore` up to 6).

### 5.5 LS04 blend slide: `blend_slide` 1.0

- **What the child does.** A written word or syllable with a mark under each part (a bar under a digraph). The child slides a finger in the course's direction and each part sounds as the finger passes it. Then a choice: the picture, or the syllable heard (sound buttons). The slide is practice; only the choice is scored. **In a scored item the whole (the word, the syllable, the merged form) plays after the choice, never before it** (decided 2026-11-06, the English teacher's point): heard first, the choice would be a listening item, and its reversed-word and letter-name distractors would no longer show a reading error. In teaching screens and at level 3 ("show me") the whole follows the parts.
- **Modes:** `graphemes` (eng: continuous sounds stretched into the next, stops clipped); `merge` (rus: the consonant tile slides to the vowel, "ммм…ма"; a stop gives only the merged syllable; in a scored item the held consonant and the vowel sound during the slide and the merged syllable after the choice); `units` (heb: CV unit by CV unit, then whole; also the "show me" of every Hebrew decoding item).
- **Schema shape:** `mode`; `word` (`text`, `parts`: each `text`, `clip`, `stretch`; `whole` clip); `answer` (`options`, `correct`) as in `tap_answer`.
- **Voices later and D2:** a part may have no clip of its own. Then, in a scored item, the parts light in silence and the whole stretched clip is the level-1 replay (credit 0.8, as "read it to me"); in a teaching screen it plays while the parts light. So the items do not change whatever the isolated-sound pilot finds (D2).
- **Classifiers** (by the distractor's tag): reversed word (`M-ENG-DIR`, `M-RUS-RTL`, `M-HEB-ORDER`); first-letter match (`M-ENG-FIRSTLETTER-GUESS`); vowel swap (`M-ENG-AE-E`, `M-HEB-VOWEL-SKIP`); a picture whose word starts with the letter's name (`M-ENG-LETTERNAME`, `M-RUS-NAME`).
- **Hint ladder:** level 1 the slide again slowly with each part lit (the parts only, never the whole); level 2 a new representation: eng the parts as tokens with mouth pictures, rus the vowel strip (LS05), heb the wheel (LS05) on the unit missed; level 3 the friend slides, says the whole and points to the answer.
- **Validator:** the parts join to the text; every grapheme is taught by the skill or below it (F5); direction is the course's; distractors tagged.

### 5.6 LS06 build a word: `build_word` 1.0

- **What the child does.** Hears a word with its picture and drags tiles from a tray into slots in the course's direction. A filled slot sounds; a tap empties it. Modes: `dictation` (heard, not shown), `copy` (shown; rus scaffold), `units` (heb CV tiles), `own_name` (heb B-27: the child's name, on the device only). `add_e` is Band C (L6).
- **Schema shape:**
  ```
  { "type": "build_word", "templateVersion": "1.0",
    "mode": "dictation | copy | units | own_name",
    "word": { "sprite": "…", "clip": "vo:…", "text": "sat" },     // none in own_name
    "tiles": [ { "text": "s", "clip": "vo:…" }, … ],            // the word's tiles plus 0 to 3 extra
    "answer": { "exact": [0,1,2],
                "soundRight": [[…]],                            // closed list, written by the teacher
                "tagged": [ { "tiles": […], "tag": "…" } ] },   // named wrong spellings
    "extraTags": { "3": "eng.lis.ae_e" },
    "finals": "auto | check" }                                  // heb; auto in Band B
  ```
- **Three outcomes** (frame §3.3.5): **exact**; **sound-right** (in the item's closed list); **not yet**. The skill's `soundRight` field (`accepts` or `teaches`, built in L0) decides the credit. A sound-right answer hears "every sound is there!" and then sees the book spelling (from the step the course introduces it). It is never called wrong.
- **Computed classifiers** on the author's data only: the exact tiles reversed → the course's DIR tag; the exact tiles less a vowel tile → `M-ENG-VOWEL-SKIP` (or `M-HEB-PARTIAL`, on the way in B-28); an extra tile used → its tag. Nothing is worked out from a dictionary at runtime.
- **The `detail` field** (`exact`, `sound_right`) is stored and synced from this step (L0 D-10 deferred it here): an enum on the answer, for the parent view ("spells every sound; learning book spelling").
- **Response code:** tile indexes in slot order (`0.3.1.2`). `own_name` writes `built` or nothing, never a tile code.
- **Hint ladder:** level 1 the word again slowly while the first empty slot sounds; level 2 a new representation: LS03 tokens above the slots, one per sound, then the tiles (heb `units`: the word split into CV units); level 3 the friend builds the word, then the slots empty and the child builds it.
- **Validator:** the exact tiles spell `word.text`; every sound-right spelling differs from the exact one and is in the course's alphabet; every extra tile is tagged; every tag is in the catalogue; decodable at the skill; `own_name` has no word, no tiles and no clips, is never a probe and never a check, and the device fills it from the parent-typed name with marks stripped (as HEB-A-11), with a short word as the fallback when no name is set.

### 5.7 LS08 `follow` and `echo`

- **`follow`** (heb B-18; Band A A-12): a page with its text shown; each word is a tap zone; the voice reads one word, waits for the child's tap on it, then the next. Words carry their own clip ids (`words: [{ text, clip }]`), so no word timing is needed. Distractors: a tap on a word past the next one (`M-HEB-ORDER`), a tap on the picture (`M-HEB-PRINT-PICTURE`). Ladder: level 1 the reading arrow over the line; level 2 the line read while a finger moves under the words; level 3 the finger stops on the next word.
- **`echo`** (rus B-08): the voice reads a phrase; a pause; the child repeats it. With the language's voice switch on and the model released, the say step of L3 runs on the key words (confident or unsure, capped evidence for the SPK skill only); otherwise an "I said it" tap. Unscored apart from that. The picture order of B-08 is a scored LS01 sequence.

### 5.8 Co-play cards (a document kind)

`card/v1`: `id`, `course`, `skills`, `kind` (`coplay`, `pencil`), a Hebrew text (`needs-editor`), an optional picture. Shown in the parent area after a bundle or a story, never voiced, never shown to the child. English: a card after each letter bundle and story from B-16 (D9). Hebrew: a pencil card for every handwriting skill (D23). Russian: a card per letter group (proposed; the teacher decides).

### 5.9 Hebrew decoding items borrow two templates for their hints

Every Hebrew decoding item (LS01 text stem in B-19 to B-23, and on into Band C) has level 2 = the LS05 wheel on the unit the child missed and level 3 = the LS04 `units` demonstration of the word (heb `templates.md` §3). The hint ladder must be able to open another template's screen as a hint picture: `hints.level2: { "show": "change_part", … }`, `hints.level3: { "show": "blend_slide", … }`. The same mechanism serves the Russian `M-RUS-HARDSOFT` re-teach (the vowel strip) and the English `M-ENG-DIR` re-teach (the slide). This is code for the app-developer inside L5-4 and L5-5.

## 7. Voices come later: how this plan works with no audio

1. **Every spoken line is written now**, as text with a clip id: Hebrew frame lines (marked `"review": "needs-editor"` until the hebrew-text-editor makes them `text-ready`), target words, sounds, syllables, story pages, slow versions for hint level 1.
2. **Every clip gets a row** in the course's `pronunciation.csv` (status `draft`: IPA, stress, traps, what the listener must hear). Hebrew rows also go into the master table (`docs/review/hebrew-master.csv`) through the editor.
3. **The pack lists every clip in `voPending`.** The app plays as it does today: a pending clip is silent, the line's bubble and chip show, and a template that waits for a clip's end waits a fixed pause (L4 D-5). Every template is tested with every clip pending.
4. **Letter sounds and the D2 pilot.** Whether a voice can say an isolated sound cleanly is not known (L2's pilot has not run). Items name a clip id, not a form. The pronunciation row says both forms (the isolated sound, and the stretched word as the D2 fallback). The pilot picks one later; no item changes.
5. **No paid step.** The voice-and-sound-director may run only the free commands (`voice_gen plan`, `audition-batch plan`, `mock`, `list`, `pack`) to count characters. The owner runs any paid command himself, later.
6. **SEC-L1-7** proposed refusing `voPending` clips in a language pack at L2. That must wait until the owner voices a course, or the Band B packs cannot be built (hand-off in §14 item 10).
7. **What cannot be checked without audio:** the listening checks, the isolated sounds, the pause lengths of hints, and whether a slide's light keeps time with a voice. These are done after voicing.

## 11. Security and child-safety items (for the security-engineer's review)

1. **Stroke data in a pack** is parsed content: bounds checked (points in the 100 box, 2 to 60 points a stroke, at most 6 strokes a form), refused when unreviewed. The child's strokes are compared in memory and never stored, logged or synced.
2. **Own name** (LS06 `own_name`, LS02 `source: own_name`): the letters come from the parent-typed name on the device, marks stripped; nothing of the name enters an attempt, the log, the sync or a hotword list; the response is `built` or empty.
3. **`detail` on the wire:** an additive enum (`exact`, `sound_right`) in the answer event; the server pattern widens, the gateway's four checks stay; deployed before the app that sends it.
4. **The microphone in `echo`:** only on a tap, only with the language's own switch on and the model released (`SpeechModelSpec.released` stays false); nothing recorded or kept; speech evidence capped and only for SPK skills.
5. **Rule 5:** every line is authored; the tutor stays off for every language item.
6. **Child safety:** no timer, countdown or speed anywhere in the new files (the source rule covers them); a missed stroke is never red; a sound-right spelling is never "wrong"; co-play cards are parent-only.
7. **No new package, no new host,** `network_hosts.json` unchanged.

## 13. Questions for the owner (each with my recommendation)

| # | Question | My recommendation and why |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | **How is a writing skill mastered?** The three syllabi split writing three different ways (English one skill with three modes; Hebrew trace, copy and memory as three skills; Russian trace and copy-plus-memory as two), and a skill done only on LS02 can never show the "two representations" the mastery rule asks for. | **One rule for all three courses (E-1):** tracing is practice; copy counts like a hinted answer; memory counts in full; mastery needs memory-mode successes on two days, one cued by the letter's **sound** and one by its **name**, and those two cues stand for the two representations. A trace-only skill (Hebrew B-24, Russian B-24) counts as met once practised and never holds a child back. This keeps D4 (memory writing is what counts) and D16 (sound and name together), and matches the evidence that free writing, not tracing, builds letter knowledge (James and Engelhardt 2012). |
| **Q2** | Is **stroke order** scored when a child writes from memory? | **No.** The start point, the direction and the shape are scored; order is shown once after a success. A fixed order did not help symbol learning and hindered it under 4½ (Merritt et al. 2020); the English and Russian models also say direction matters more than order. |
| **Q3** | Build a **pilot slice** (the first letter group of each course, about 40 items each) with the templates, before the full batches? | **Yes.** The templates are then tested on the content they must carry, and a wrong turn costs 40 items, not 400. |
| **Q4** | The English **alphabet song** (B-22) and the Hebrew **letter-name song**: spoken chants for now? | **Yes, chants** (your decision of 2026-11-05). A chant on a beat carries the order of the names; a tune can be added later without changing the items. |
| **Q5** | Russian **B-02 and B-07** are listed for LS07 (build a sentence), which is Band C work. Serve them with LS01 now and add LS07 items in L6? | **Yes.** Their syllabus rows ask for "choose the phrase" and "sort by ending", which LS01 does; building LS07 early would delay all three courses. |
| **Q6** | **Co-play and pencil cards:** on the parent's screen only, never voiced? | **Yes.** They are for the adult; voicing them costs characters for no teaching gain. |
| **Q7** | **Should Band B reach the children before its sounds are voiced?** | **No family release of a letter or sound sub-batch before its clips are voiced.** A silent letter-sound item teaches nothing, and a child left to guess a sound may learn the letter's name as its sound (the NAME misconception). | **Q8** | Write Band B in **sub-batches** (three for Hebrew, two for English and Russian), each validated, tested and packed on its own? | **Yes.** Smaller reviews for the editor and the teachers, QA once per sub-batch, and the courses never wait for each other (D5). |
