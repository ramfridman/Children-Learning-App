## 3. The new lesson shape (a shared rule)

A lesson on the new-learner route has four parts:

1. **Recall** (1 to 2 scored items): words met in earlier lessons. Lesson 1 opens with the hello routine instead.
2. **Meet** (2 to 3 new words, never more), one screen per word: the picture; the word; the Hebrew meaning once (eng, rus); the word again; "let's say it together" and the word; the child says it or taps "I said it". Touching the picture plays the word again. No right or wrong.
3. **Find** (at least 2 items per new word): first **2 choices**, then 3. Every picture is a word already met. The two closest words (мама/папа) are not each other's first partner. A wrong tap: the picture says its own word, then "and where is …?" and the target.
4. **Use** (1 to 3 items): say it (LS09), listen and do (LS11), a phrase or chant (LS10, LS12).

Each new word is heard 8 to 10 times in its lesson, returns in the Recall of one of the next 3 lessons, then in revisits. Heritage children keep their route (no Meet screens; the hearing screens of `band-a-build.md` §2.6.1). Why: spaced retrieval builds young children's words (Childers and Tomasello 2002; Vlach and others 2008), but only once the word has a meaning to retrieve; the gain from guessing first is shown in adults, not in preschoolers guessing among pictures of unheard words (Kornell and others 2009).

### 3.1 English, lesson 1 for a complete beginner (every clip exists)

- **Meet** `hello` (picture `pip-arrive`): "hello", "בְּעִבְרִית:" + `he-gl-hello` (שָׁלוֹם), "hello", "בּוֹאוּ נֹאמַר יַחַד" + "hello". Then `goodbye` (`pip-leave`), the same.
- **Find**, 2 choices: hello, `pip-arrive`/`pip-leave` (i01); goodbye, the reverse (i02 without `mika-eat`); both again with Mika (`mika-arrive`/`mika-leave`, i05 and a twin): the same words on a new character.
- **Use**: say "hello" as Pip arrives (LS09); Pip's bubble, hello or goodbye (i07); the hello chant (r01, unscored).
- `hi` and `bye` come later as "another way to say it", never as each other's wrong answer. 3 choices start in lesson 2.

### 3.2 Russian, the first family lesson

- **Recall**: привет / пока.
- **Meet** мама (`fam-mother`, "בְּעִבְרִית: אִמָּא"), бабушка (`fam-grandmother`, סָבְתָא), папа (`fam-father`, אַבָּא), in that order, so мама and папа are not met back to back.
- **Find**: мама/бабушка, папа/бабушка; then all three (items 01, 02 of `02-family`). The close pair as two spoken options (item 12) waits for the next lesson's Recall.
- **Use**: say each word (the existing echo); "Это мама" (`ch-eto-mama`) with the puppet pointing. дедушка comes next lesson.

### 3.3 Hebrew

The Hebrew course is for Hebrew speakers, so the rule applies to its **new** words (story words, the A-02 verbs) and its stories: each story plays **before** the lesson that asks its words; each new word gets a **Meet page** (the story picture, the word twice, one short Hebrew sentence that explains it; Biemiller and Boote 2006); a wrong picture is a word already met or an everyday word the teacher marks `everyday` (a sound-alike must be everyday). Story 3 stays a transfer check; the Hebrew teacher decides whether it also gets a listening lesson.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

1. **Beginner start.** In each course a child whose parent answered "nothing", or nothing at all, opens lesson 1 with no probe; "some" gets the placement as today; the Russian sounds strand only under its condition.
2. **Headless playthrough of lesson 1** in each course (new learner, no answers, clips pending): the parts come as Recall (or the hello routine), Meet, Find, Use; **no scored item asks a word that was not met first, and no picture on a scored screen is a word not met**; each new word's first Find has 2 choices; each new word is heard at least 8 times; in English and Russian the Hebrew meaning is said exactly once per new word, on its Meet screen.
3. **Validator rule** (L7-5) passes on the three real courses and refuses each broken fixture with a named reason.
4. **Evidence.** A wrong tap in the introduction visit writes no tag and no `incorrect`; the same item in a later visit, or for a heritage child, is scored as usual; the parent view shows nothing from an introduction visit.
5. **Feedback.** A tapped picture says its word; the first wrong tap says the tapped word, then the target.
6. **Hebrew.** Every story plays before any lesson that asks its words; every new word has a Meet page.
7. **Letters by age, level first.** From 3;0 to 4;11 a beginner is served no letter skill in any course, whatever the presentation band; a child of that age whose placement confirms letters or reading (or, in Russian, whose parent turned on "letters before 5") is served them; Hebrew letters open at 5;0; English and Russian at 5 only through their gate; "letters together with words" is hidden under 6, on by default at 7, and never opens before the early set.
8. **Mastery.** Every skill of the re-cut courses reaches fresh mastery in the simulator.
9. **Texts and packs.** New Hebrew lines `text-ready`; three packs built, validated, signed; analyzer, format and the whole suite green.
